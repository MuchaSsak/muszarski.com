Round 3 · your notes

What changed, note by note

"You can't easily tell which technologies I'm using… monochrome silhouettes?"

Tools now appear as one-colour logos, always with the name next to them:
  • a running band under the hero, with a pause button
  • logos on every project
  • a Stack section where coloured squares show which project used each tool; tap a project to light up its tools

"More icons and less overwhelming text."

Fact lists became icon tiles, paragraphs became one-liners, and links got icons. The longest text left on the page is the hero sentence.

"Showcase projects with the videos… placeholder video components."

Each project gets a video player:
  • a frame that matches the platform: browser, phone or monitor
  • chapter chips that jump through the video and fill as it plays
  • play/pause and full-screen
Videos load only when you scroll near them and play only when half visible. PstrykWeb and Clockful show a drawn stand-in marked "Video soon" until you send recordings.

"Award PstrykWeb as #1, latest and my favourite commercial project… the Order website component only if nicely animated."

PstrykWeb gets:
  • a #1 badge on its screen: "Latest · my favourite · commercial"
  • a three-step "how it works" row
  • the order card as a 10-second loop: the form fills in, the cursor presses Order, the card flies off, and the site builds and gets a LIVE stamp
Once the real video exists, the card plays over its first seconds and then gets out of the way.

"Rebuild How I work… product engineer… business, conversions, clients."

"Product engineer" set huge, then a ring of seven stops: Client, Users, Conversion, Revenue, Code, Ship, Measure. A dot travels the ring, and each stop has a one-line idea and proof from a real project. Code is one stop of seven.

"Typechecks and lints are the floor… focus on automation, LLM wiki, MCPs, skills."

"Delegate. Automate." follows one real task through the setup this site is built with: a prompt, 3 hooks, 33 wiki pages, subagents, 3 MCP servers, 7 skills and 27 lessons. A small label travels along the steps and shows what each one adds. The checks are now one line: "That's the floor, not the pitch."

"Credentials: sneak previews, hover effects that expand and show pictures… without loading it all."

Cards open on hover, tap or Enter. Their video or image loads only when a card opens near the screen, and pauses when it closes. The state qualifications get three drawn stamps, and Three.js Journey plays your own space site.

"Do we even need YOUR EMAIL? Can't it be just message?"

The message box comes first and stands alone. A "Where do I reply?" field appears once you start writing. It takes an email, a phone number or LinkedIn, and fills itself if the message already contains one. Without any contact you couldn't answer, so the field stays, but it comes late and takes little effort.

"Mobile always, no cursors… premium on all platforms."

Everything that reacts to hover also works with a tap, the keyboard or scrolling. On phones:
  • the AI steps light up as you scroll past them
  • credentials become an accordion
  • video controls fit one row

Round 3 · your call

Four answers, then I build

  1. PstrykWeb stack. You asked for the full stack list, but your public-info rule keeps the internal part off the site. Should I list it, or keep public tech only? (I sent you the names in chat.)

    Right now it shows Next.js, React, TypeScript and Vercel.

  2. Contact. The message comes first, and a reply field appears once you write and fills itself from the message. Is that OK?

    Without a reply field you'd get messages you can't answer.

  3. Recordings. I need three recordings from you. The chips on each player are the shot list.
    • PstrykWeb: the public konfigurator, from filling it in to the result (about 20 s).
    • Clockful: timer, entries, reports, roles. This one needs Neoteric's OK.
    • Linkoglot: a full-size screen recording. Today's is the 332 px store preview.
  4. Credentials media. Your certificates and your own photos (Erasmus+, hackathons) go in the open cards.

    The Three.js Journey course video belongs to Bruno Simon, so it needs his written OK. Until then the card shows your own space site, built with what the course taught.

Theme and font: I kept Cobalt with a Paper/Ink toggle, and Anybody, since you didn't object. Say if you want something else.

What the comp does not prove yet

  • It hasn't been tested on a real phone, only on emulated 390 and 1440 px screens.
  • There's no Lighthouse run on the comp; the real build gets one per section.
  • The screenshot browser can't play these videos, so playback isn't in the shots. The order card over a real video needs the recording.

Not real yet

  • The "Video soon" players for PstrykWeb and Clockful, and the photo and certificate slots in Credentials.
  • The email, the links and the CV size are placeholders, and the form sends nothing.
  • "Cetuspro" and "Neoteric" wait for their permission.
  • The AI numbers are counted from this repo today and get refreshed at build time.
  • The tool logos come from Simple Icons (CC0) and remain their brands' trademarks. They are shown in one colour, unaltered, only to name tools you use. I'll check each brand's guidelines before launch.
  • The other icons are from Tabler (MIT).
