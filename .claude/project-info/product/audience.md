# Audience

> Owns: who the site must convince, what they check, their objections and the evidence that answers each.
> Evidence: `research/2026-10-05-software-house-hiring.md` (31 current PL offers read 2026-10-05),
> `research/2026-10-05-hiring-perspective.md`. How we talk to them: `brand.md`.

## Primary segment: Polish software houses (+ product companies)

| Reader | Arrives from | Checks in 5–30 s | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter / talent partner | LinkedIn, CV link, referral | role, location, availability, contract (B2B 29/31 offers), CV PDF that parses in an ATS (7/11 career pages use one) | [research] |
| Tech lead / CTO | recruiter hand-off, GitHub | one case study: scope, decisions, how AI output is verified, code | [research] |
| End client (software houses often add a client interview) | the software house | outcomes first, in English, non-technical | [research] |

**What PL offers ask for in 2026** (n = 31) [research]:
- **The "product engineer" profile, word for word:** ownership / end-to-end 21, client or stakeholder contact 21,
  communication 16, risks / ask early 7, trade-offs 5.
- **Process signals:** code review 11, tests 16, CI/CD 16. Agile 3, Jira 0, Scrum 0, degree 0.
- **AI-assisted development:** required in 16, a plus in 2, restricted in 0. The strong versions pair it with accountability
  ("explain AI-generated code", "hands-on coding without AI").
- **Interviews** now test the judgment and verification of AI output (CoderPad 2026, Karat 2026, Canva).

## Secondary segments

- **Freelance clients** (the "open to projects" line) [user]: they need outcome, trust, a quick contact.
- **Search visitors:** name, project names, blog topics (`seo.md`).
- **AI assistants and screeners:** server-rendered facts, `/llms.txt`.

## Not for

Recruiters looking for a "Senior" title by years alone. Studios hiring pure motion/3D artists (only 10 three.js
offers in PL) [research]. 3D is shown as craft, not as the job title.

## Seniority framing [research § 5; open values → open-questions Q25]

- **Split the years honestly:** "Building software since 2022 · <N> years of paid client/contract work ·
  <N> products shipped". ~4 years in total [user 2026-10-05]; freelance and the Cetuspro contract count as commercial.
- **The 4-year story is a strength, not a gap:** he started before technikum and has already shipped client work,
  two store apps and an internship build. Lead with shipped products, never with a years count.
- **Honest strong numbers:** systems in production with dates, stores published, locales, Lighthouse of this site.
- **Never:** Linkoglot users or revenue (there are none yet; say "pre-traction"), invented percentages.
