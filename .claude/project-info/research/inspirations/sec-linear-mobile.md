# Linear Mobile: a portrait app in a landscape slot + device-aware CTA

Date: 2026-10-05 · URL: https://linear.app/mobile · Section: mixed aspect ratios (c) · Screens: `img/sec-linear-mobile-desktop.jpg`, `img/sec-linear-mobile-mobile.jpg`

- **What works:**
  - A desaturated, grainy photo of the phone in a hand fills a 16:9 slab, so the portrait app sits in the landscape grid.
  - Desktop shows **QR "Scan to download" + App Store / Play tiles**; mobile shows one "Download on the App Store" button.
- **Measured:** 2.5 MB first load; H1 in HTML; 3 sticky elements.
- **Steal:** a QR on desktop and a store badge on phones (Linkoglot). Normalise the slot, not the media.
- **Skip:** the photo-in-hand look itself. Under Drafting Table, use a multi-view phone drawing with detail callouts instead.
