## Visual style
- Dark, cinematic: a **grainy black-to-grey radial gradient** with a portrait whose face is **dissolved into red pixel-sorting / datamosh** (WebGL/canvas) — strong, slightly unsettling art direction.
- Centre caption in light grotesk: "Ravi Klaassens. Design & Code for those who refuse to settle. 2024—Future", framed by four tiny corner squares.
- Logo: a white knight-on-horse silhouette. Live clock "10:06:12 CEST" in a boxed chip.

## Navigation
- Desktop: a **grouped mega-nav, always open** across the top: *Repertoire* (Works, Insights) · *Narrative* (About, Brand story, Connect) · *Liaison* (For Brands, For Agencies) — audience-segmented entry points.
- Floating bottom-left **"Get in touch →"** pill in the hero; after scrolling, a **compact bottom-centre dock** appears (logo · "Directory ≡" · "Sound") — the mega-nav collapses into a persistent mini menu (desktop-2 shot).
- Real routes: `/about`, `/contact`, `/for-agencies`, `/for-brands`, `/insights/<slug>`, `/archives/<slug>` [measured].

## How projects are shown
Grid of large art-directed images, each captioned **name + discipline** ("Buyt Bags — Web Development", "R—K — Identity + Art Direction"). Case studies under `/archives/<slug>` (R-K, Prop, Buyt, TA Design, MFK, Chris Hansen) + Insights articles (process, rebrand timing) — content beyond visuals.

## Mobile
Works; the hero portrait fills the screen, "Get in touch →" centred near the bottom; nav reduced to "Discover" + vertical progress line (top-right). Less obvious wayfinding than desktop.

## Preloader / weight [measured]
Preloader ~6–8 s on desktop in my run; DCL 1.2 s. 3.4 MB / 43 requests desktop; 1.4 MB mobile.

## Steal
- **Audience-split entry points** (For Brands / For Agencies) → for a job hunt: "For Recruiters / For Engineers" (CV & stack vs code & write-ups).
- **Grouped, labelled nav in the hero that collapses into a bottom dock** once you scroll — menu never more than one click away.
- **Discipline tag under every project** ("Web Development") — tells a recruiter your role at a glance.
- **Insights/articles** next to work — proves thinking.

## Skip
- 6–10 s preloader before anything is clickable.
- Mobile nav reduced to an ambiguous "Discover" label.
