# Stripe Press: fixed slots, free paint

Date: 2026-10-05 · URL: https://press.stripe.com/ · Section: per-project theming (b) · Screens: `img/sec-stripe-press-desktop.jpg`, `img/sec-stripe-press-mobile.jpg`

- **What works:**
  - Every book spine has completely different art: marbled paper, pixel weave, kintsugi cracks, engraving.
  - The spine *slots* never change: author left, title centre, logo right, one serif family, same proportions.
  - The collection feels like one object.
- **Measured:** WebGL (2 canvases), 9.06 MB first load. On mobile the 3D stack is just cropped (weak). H1 in HTML.
- **Steal:** the coherence rule for Drafting Table sheets. The title block, line weights and type are fixed; paper, ink, accent and motif vary.
- **Skip:** the WebGL spines and their weight; the mobile crop.
