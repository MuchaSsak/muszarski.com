## Visual style
- Near-black background, white text, tiny violet bullet dots as section markers.
- The top band is a **5-column dossier in monospace** (INTRO · POSITION · RECOGNITION · BRANDS · CONNECT): avatar, birth year, location + live local time, client-portfolio stats ("$1B+ client revenue"), average project cost ranges in £/€, previous employers, award counts, brand list, availability ("Available September 2026").
- Under it, a large sans statement ("An award-winning WordPress developer and delivery partner…") that cross-fades between lines on scroll. An underlined "agencies¹⁷" hover term shows a superscript count.
- Bottom: a horizontal strip of grayscale project screenshots plus a pill toggle **Projects ① / Awards ②**.

## Navigation
- No menu. The whole proposition sits on screen one. The first viewport has 9 actionable links, including mailto with a **pre-filled enquiry template** (name/company/scope/budget/timeline/timezone in the body) [measured].
- Single page about 4 screens tall [measured]. No per-project URLs: projects are screenshots in a scroll-driven strip.

## How projects are shown
A strip of large screenshot cards (Bay Harbor Towers, Bedford Square, "Disrupting Industries", "Engineering the future of aging medicine", Eight80…). They read as visual proof, not case studies. A tab switches the strip to Awards.

## Mobile
Works. The dossier collapses behind **"CREDENTIALS OPEN[+]"** (progressive disclosure). The statement leads, then a swipeable project carousel, then CONNECT. The Projects/Awards pill stays bottom-centre like an app tab bar.

## Preloader / weight [measured]
No preloader. Desktop: 0.20 MB at load, 1.17 MB after scroll (mostly fonts, 0.81 MB across several families). Load event 1.15 s.

## Steal
- **Recruiter dossier above the fold**, rewritten for a job hunt: role, location + timezone, availability date, stack, years, notable employers/clients, awards, and contact actions, all scannable in 10 s.
- **Pre-filled mailto template.** For a job seeker: "Hi <name>, I'm hiring for <role>…". It lowers friction and raises reply quality.
- **Collapsible credentials on mobile** ("OPEN[+]") instead of a hamburger.
- **Bottom pill tab bar** (Projects/Awards) for one-thumb switching on mobile.

## Skip
- Publishing price ranges. Relevant for freelancers, wrong for job seekers.
- Screenshots without per-project pages: recruiters can't see the problem, your role, or the outcome.
- Several font families (0.81 MB of fonts). Subset or cut to 2.
